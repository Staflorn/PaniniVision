## **About**

This mod replaces Minecraft’s standard camera projection with Panini Projection, helping reduce the “fisheye” effect and image stretching near the edges of the screen when using a high field of view.

Panini Projection preserves the feeling of a wide FOV while producing a more natural-looking image. Vertical lines remain less distorted, and objects near the edges of the screen appear closer to their normal shape. The effect is especially noticeable in first-person gameplay, with high FOV settings, and on ultrawide monitors.

## **Features**

- Replaces Minecraft’s standard camera projection with Panini Projection.
- Reduces the “fisheye” effect and edge distortion.
- Supports third-party shaders.
- Works with compatible shader packs used in your Minecraft setup.
- Allows you to adjust the strength of the projection effect.
Suitable for ultrawide monitors and players who prefer a high FOV.

## **Who Is This Mod For?**

This mod is intended for players who enjoy using a high FOV but dislike the strong distortion around the edges of the screen. It may also be useful for ultrawide monitor users and players who use third-party shaders but want a more comfortable and natural-looking perspective in Minecraft.

## **Notes**

**By default, the hotkey for configuring the mod is assigned to the left arrow key, but you can change this in the control settings.**

During development, I studied the Panini Projection implementation in Sodium Extra to better understand possible approaches to integrating this technology. The Sodium Extra code was not copied or directly reused - the mod was implemented independently with a focus on compatibility with third-party shaders.
